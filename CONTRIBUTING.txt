CONTRIBUTING

1 SCOPE

The submission scope is defined in documentation/AFP.txt. Contributions
shall preserve that scope unless the project owner approves a change.
Research extensions belong in thys/Research and shall not become submission
dependencies without an explicit scope decision.

2 SOURCES

Edit the maintained sources in thys. submission/ROOT is the export manifest.
Do not edit generated archives or extracted copies. Keep theory names,
session membership, document references, and public theorem interfaces
consistent with a move or rename.

3 VERIFICATION

Run the repository source check and the affected Isabelle sessions. Changes
to the submission require export and verification of that archive. Proof,
style, and document checks are specified in documentation/BUILD.txt.
A verification claim shall identify the revision and command used.

4 PRESENTATION

Follow AGENTS.md for source structure, naming, and records. Describe the
mathematical object, assumptions, result, and limitations. Public text shall
not contain private correspondence, conversational context, or unsupported
priority and completion claims.

5 PUBLICATION

Retain applicable attribution and license notices. Record substantial AI
assistance accurately. Only the project owner may authorize AFP submission
or contact with reviewers. A verified candidate is not an accepted entry.
