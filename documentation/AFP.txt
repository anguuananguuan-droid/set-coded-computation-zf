1 THEOREM AND BOUNDARY

Set_Coded_Halting_Invariance_ZF constructs an explicit internal halting
formula for set-coded Turing machines and proves its satisfaction theorem
in every transitive ZFC set model. It constructs two primitive-recursive
many-one reductions from self-input halting and its complement to the
natural-number codes of invariant closed sentences.

The direct semantic premise is that CH is not invariant across transitive
ZFC set models. A countable transitive ZFC set model supplies this premise
through Independence_CH. The target quantifies over all transitive ZFC set
models, not only the countable ones.

The source halting set is proved undecidable in the defined machine model.
The reducers are certified in Isabelle/ZF's prim_rec class and evaluated on
singleton lists. The entry does not establish closure of machine decidability
under arbitrary primitive-recursive reductions. It therefore does not claim
machine undecidability of the full target set, a general machine compiler,
or the EPQ arithmetic-truth reduction.

4 FORMAL CONTRIBUTION

The classical inputs are halting diagonalisation, absoluteness for
transitive models, and the independence of CH. The formal contribution is
the explicit connection from set-coded execution through internal
satisfaction and primitive-recursive sentence construction to invariance.
No claim of a new classical theorem or priority is made.
