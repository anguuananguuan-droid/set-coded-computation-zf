AFP SUBMISSION

1 SCOPE

The candidate entry is Set_Coded_Halting_Invariance_ZF. It constructs an
explicit internal halting formula for set-coded Turing machines, proves its
satisfaction theorem in every transitive ZFC set model, and constructs
primitive-recursive many-one reductions from self-input halting and its
complement to the codes of invariant closed sentences. The reductions
hold when CH is not invariant. The stated countable transitive ZFC set
model assumption supplies this premise through Independence_CH.

The source halting set is proved undecidable in the machine model through
a concrete rejection transformation. The entry does not establish closure
of machine decidability under arbitrary primitive-recursive reductions.
It therefore does not claim machine undecidability of the full target set,
a general compiler, or the EPQ arithmetic-truth reduction.

2 SUBMISSION UNIT

submission/ROOT lists the nineteen theories included in the entry. They are
maintained in Turing_Machines_ZF/Core and Set_Coded_Invariance_ZF. The export
copies those sources and makes their computation-library imports local to
one session. It includes the proof document and the repository license.
No separate unaccepted computation entry is required.

The export alters only the declared computation-library imports. Archive
member order, permissions, ownership, and timestamps are normalized. In
the same Python environment, unchanged submission contents produce the
same archive bytes without embedding the exporting user's identity.

The numeric evaluator, tape observation, workspace preservation, concrete
programs, and arithmetic-truth extensions remain in the research repository.
They are not included in this submission. Instruction-code normalization is
shared with the evaluator through a separate theory.

3 REPRODUCTION

Use Isabelle2025-2 and the AFP snapshot dated 6 February 2026. Register the
AFP theory directory as specified in documentation/BUILD.txt. Then export
and extract the submission from the repository root.

    bash scripts/package-afp-entry.sh
    mkdir -p output/afp-entry
    tar -xzf output/Set_Coded_Halting_Invariance_ZF.tar.gz -C output/afp-entry
    isabelle build -v -c -D output/afp-entry/Set_Coded_Halting_Invariance_ZF

With LuaLaTeX installed, include both proof-document variants.

    isabelle build -v -c -o browser_info -o document=pdf -o 'document_variants=document:outline=/proof,/ML' -D output/afp-entry/Set_Coded_Halting_Invariance_ZF

The archive is the verification target. The development session layout is
not the submission layout. CI builds the extracted archive and publishes
the archive, proof document, outline, mandatory style report, and build
logs together. Proof dependencies and AFP mandatory checks are inspected
using the commands in documentation/BUILD.txt.

4 REVIEW

The classical inputs are halting diagonalisation, absoluteness for
transitive models, and the independence of CH. The formal contribution is
the explicit connection from this machine semantics through internal
satisfaction and primitive-recursive sentence construction to invariance.
No claim of a new classical theorem or priority is made.

The entry is a candidate. AFP acceptance and external specialist review
have not been established. The author must review the submission metadata,
confirm the maintenance commitment, and submit through the AFP process.
