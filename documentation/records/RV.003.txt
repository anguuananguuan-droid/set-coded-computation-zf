2 Mathematical findings

2.1 No defect invalidating the stated main results was identified. The
machine decoder is total on natural numbers and recovers every canonically
encoded machine. The concrete rejection transformation discharges the
self-input halting diagonal argument without a general compiler.

2.2 The internal halting theorem is proved from transitivity and ZFC
satisfaction. Its correctness is not a locale assumption. Model closure
places the canonical finite execution witnesses inside the model. Naming
formulas then bind both machine and input, producing a closed sentence with
the proved external halting truth value.

2.3 Formula coding is injective on formulas. The target contains exactly
codes of invariant closed formulas. Invalid codes and codes of open formulas
do not acquire membership through a second formula witness. The effective
maps have exact application equations for the intended formula codes and
are members of the total function class prim_rec. Instruction normalization
handles noncanonical source codes. The bounded traversal covers every
decoded machine instruction.

2.4 Invariance quantifies over all transitive ZFC set models. The countable
transitive model premise supplies two witnesses that disagree on CH. These
witnesses suffice to refute invariance in the larger model class. Without
any transitive ZFC models, every closed sentence is invariant vacuously.
The disagreement premise cannot be discarded.

3 Revisions

3.1 The naming headings incorrectly described formulas of arity one as
closed names. They now identify names for natural numbers and finite lists.
The halting introduction also now distinguishes arbitrary witness sets
with prescribed finite applications from the canonical finite functions
constructed by the proof. Formula definitions and satisfaction statements
are unchanged.

3.2 The two effective reductions now have interfaces requiring directly
that CH is not invariant. Existing countable transitive model statements
retain their names and propositions as consequences of CH_not_invariant.
The numerical maps and their application equations are unchanged. This
separates the effective construction from the model existence argument.

3.3 The primitive recursive reduction relation now has total output,
reflexivity, composition, and transitivity lemmas. Composition uses the
existing COMP constructor. These lemmas expose the reusable reduction
interface without adding a machine realization assumption.

3.4 The proof document now credits Transitive Models of Fragments of ZFC
for the model closure, relativization, and satisfaction infrastructure.
The previous citations to the CH and Turing machine entries remain.

3.5 Export changes are restricted to the theory import declarations.
Qualified imports of excluded local theories are rejected. Archive ordering,
permissions, ownership, and timestamps are normalized. The export no longer
embeds the exporting user's name or identifier.

3.6 Continuous integration now preserves the submission heap, checks the
actual theorem proof dependencies, and runs the AFP mandatory linter at
revision c9983adc5c51a6455f702bb58c9f960d425a2af3. The checks and their reports
remain outside the submission theories. A failure rejects the run.

5 Remaining boundary

5.1 The completed endpoint is the pair of primitive recursive reductions
into the codes of invariant closed sentences. No general primitive recursive
function compiler for this machine model, machine undecidability theorem
for the full target set, arbitrary arithmetic truth reduction, or precise
complexity classification of that set is claimed.

5.2 This review does not establish mathematical novelty, independent
specialist approval, AFP submission, or AFP acceptance. The classical inputs
are known results. The candidate contribution is their explicit formal
connection through set coded execution, internal satisfaction, and effective
sentence construction. External review and the author's submission and
maintenance decisions remain separate obligations.
