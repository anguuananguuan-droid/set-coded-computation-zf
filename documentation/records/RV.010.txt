2 ESTABLISHED INTERFACES

Turing_Halting_Formula proves membership of halt_fm in formula, its arity,
and its satisfaction theorem. Turing_Halting_Sentences proves the closed
sentence interface in transitive ZF set models. Turing_Halting_Codes proves
primitive-recursive construction of the corresponding formula codes.
These are proved constructions, not assumed halting interfaces.

Turing_Rejection proves self_halting_not_tm_decidable.
Turing_Effective_Invariance proves primitive-recursive reductions from
self-input halting and its complement to invariant_formula_codes.
Its generic theorem assumes uniform truth, closed sentence syntax,
primitive-recursive sentence codes, and a non-invariant closed sentence.
The CH corollaries obtain non-invariance from a countable transitive ZFC
set model. They do not assert existence of that model.

Turing_Evaluator and Turing_Evaluator_Primrec prove numerical simulation
and primitive-recursive certificates. Turing_Composition proves sequential
machine composition. Turing_Reduction proves preservation of decidability
under machine-realised many-one reductions. Turing_Decidable_Sets proves
membership of machine-decidable sets in transitive ZFC set models.

3 OPEN OBLIGATIONS

Turing_Arithmetic_Truth provides atomic arithmetic relations and restricted
quantifiers. It does not provide a general arithmetic syntax, standard
truth definition for that syntax, recursive translation, structural
truth-preservation proof, or certified translation of arbitrary sentence
codes. The full arithmetic-truth reduction in the EPQ paper is not proved.
The paper's further non-arithmeticity conclusion also requires the relevant
undefinability and preimage-closure theorems in the formal development.

Turing_Realisation does not prove arbitrary COMP or PREC realisation.
Turing_Primrec_Reduction explicitly assumes realisation when transferring
numerical primitive-recursive reductions to machine reductions.
Neither a general machine compiler nor a universal machine implementing
the numerical evaluator is present. The target invariance set has not
been proved undecidable for this machine model.

The EPQ translation is direct from arithmetic syntax to set-theoretic
syntax. It does not pass full arithmetic truth through ordinary halting.
A universal machine is not a prerequisite for that syntactic reduction.
The numerical reduction has a membership equivalence in both directions.
A reverse reduction from the target to the source is not needed to transfer
undecidability once the forward reducing function has a machine realiser.
