IN.002
12 October 2026
Authority Tang Ziyi
Prepared by OpenAI Codex

1 Mission

Organise the maintained repository around the explicit connection from
set coded execution through internal halting satisfaction and effective
sentence coding to model invariance. The exported AFP candidate shall
retain this single mathematical scope.

2 Source boundary

The baseline is 3701a43. Computation, invariance, and research theories shall
have distinct source directories under thys. The forty maintained theories
shall remain registered in five development sessions. The nineteen theory
submission shall be generated from the maintained sources and its manifest.

The EPQ manuscript, license notices, attribution, and historical records
shall be preserved. Private and ignored drafts are outside the public
revision. This instruction does not adopt the private collaboration draft
as a repository standard.

3 Revision scope

Current public documents shall match the maintained sources. Theory headers
and section hierarchy shall be consistent. Redundant local proofs and
historical dependencies may be removed when the public mathematical
statements remain accounted for. A changed theory namespace or removed
interface shall be identified in the completion record.

The linter compatibility failure shall be corrected with verifiable source
provenance and deliberate control cases. Proof documents shall have stable
references and remain within their declared text bounds. Prior records
shall retain their original observations. A later record shall correct
interpretations that the new evidence supersedes.

4 Acceptance and publication

The independent computation library, all development sessions, and the
extracted submission shall build. The submission shall use its declared
timeout. Proof dependencies, effective style checking, source navigation,
archive reproducibility, and both document variants shall be checked.

Repository publication is within the authorised mission. No AFP submission
or communication with editors or reviewers is authorised.
