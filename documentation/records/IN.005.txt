IN.005
TURING MACHINE LIBRARY
13 October 2026

1 MISSION

Provide a reusable Isabelle/ZF machine library with explicit execution
semantics, tape observations, and a verified internal halting interface.

2 DESIGN ESSENCE

The public object is the machine library. Its definitions, proof interfaces,
examples, and documentation shall describe the same mathematical contract.

3 TARGET WORLD

A client can define a machine, prove its finite behaviour, transfer a result
between equivalent tape representations, and use the halting satisfaction
theorem without unfolding its internal formula.

4 FROZEN INVARIANTS

Machine semantics and established theorem premises shall be preserved.
Every maintained theory shall have one source. Qualified imports shall
identify the same public namespace in the repository and archive. Research
applications shall remain available outside the candidate closure.

5 BOUNDARIES

The candidate includes numerical machine and formula codes and the existing
primitive-recursive self-input sentence constructor. It does not require a
general machine compiler, CH reduction, or full EPQ formalisation. Existing
model-library dependencies shall be stated explicitly.

6 DELIVERABLES

The candidate source, worked clients, library guide, proof document, export
manifest, and maintenance checks shall agree on scope and public names.

7 ACCEPTANCE

All maintained sessions shall build. The operational source closure shall
build without AFP. Public endpoints and the candidate proof closure shall
pass dependency checks. The exported archive shall pass proof, style, and
both document-variant checks. Evidence shall identify the checked sources.

8 ESCALATION

A change to machine semantics, theorem premises, contribution attribution,
or the authorised mathematical scope requires a new scope decision.
AFP submission requires separate explicit authorisation.
