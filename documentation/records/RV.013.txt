ARITHMETIC TRANSLATION
25 November 2026

1 SOURCE AND RESULT

This revision extends the Turing_Models research session from main at
cfb0ba09cc87cec42d17656df18f66e41011cd23.

Arithmetic_Translation.thy defines relational first-order arithmetic,
its satisfaction over nat, and a recursive translation into formula.
The proved interfaces cover formula membership, exact arity preservation,
and truth preservation under every transitive ZF set model. The result
for closed sentences uses the empty environment.

Arithmetic_Invariance.thy proves arith_or_invariant_iff. Given a closed
disagreement sentence, the disjunction of the translated arithmetic
sentence and that sentence is ZFC invariant exactly when the source
is true over nat.
