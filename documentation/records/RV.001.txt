All twenty seven versioned project theories were inventoried by session,

2 Findings

The independent session already contained eight theories, but the public
documents mixed its results with unfinished extensions. The development guide
retained a false dependency from Turing_Context to Turing_Workspace. The build
guide described three sessions despite four ROOT declarations. Turing_CH
inherited the entire extended machine session although its own imports
required only core coding and AFP theories.

The concrete self halting theorem uses machine coding, decision semantics, and
the rejection transformation. Numeric evaluation and bounded workspace
preservation are separate results. In the extended development, primitive
recursive certification does not supply a general machine compiler. In the
model application, sats_halt_fm_iff remains a locale assumption. Finite trace
membership and the arithmetic formula fragment do not discharge that
assumption or complete the EPQ reduction.
