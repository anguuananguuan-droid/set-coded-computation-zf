2 Verification

Isabelle checks derivability from the definitions and assumptions in the
source. Testing, semantic review, and independent expert review are distinct
activities. The repaired output contract and the workspace counterexample
illustrate why a checked proof can still depend on an unsuitable
specification.

Neither automated builds nor AI review establish novelty, independent expert
endorsement, or completion of the EPQ theorem. A specific claim of human
review requires its own record.
